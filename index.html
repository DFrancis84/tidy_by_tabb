Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you service?

Tidy by Tabb proudly serves the Louisville area, including Jefferson County, Oldham County, Bullitt County, Henry County, Trimble County, and Shelby County / Shelbyville.

Are you insured?

Yes. Tidy by Tabb is fully insured to provide additional peace of mind while working in your home.

Do you provide cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment. If you prefer specific products, please let us know before your appointment.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on square footage, the current condition of the home, type of cleaning requested, and any add-on services selected.

What is your cancellation policy?

More than 72 hours notice: reschedule at no charge. 48-72 hours notice: $50 fee. 24-48 hours notice: $75 fee. Less than 24 hours or same-day cancellation: 50% of the quoted service fee.

What makes Tidy by Tabb different?

We focus on dependable service, attention to detail, clear communication, personalized customer care, and cleaning plans tailored to your home’s needs.

Can I customize my cleaning service?

Absolutely. We offer customized cleaning packages designed around your home’s specific needs, priorities, and budget.
